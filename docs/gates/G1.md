## 1. Summary and recommendation

**Recommendation: proceed, subject to brief item 6.** The speech instrument (Guidepup 0.35.0 for NVDA lifecycle and input, the receive-only relay tap for speech) meets the G1 rule on `windows-2025` with retries off:

- at least 50 valid runs of each gating canary (252 in all), with **0 failures**;
- INCONCLUSIVE in 0.4% of attempts (1 of 253; limit 5%);
- tap-versus-log parity exact per segment: 553 against 553 over 443 segments.

The zero failures rest on the canary speech-matching rule of DR-0048, adopted after the smoke run, because NVDA's speech dictionaries rewrite "K1" as "K 1". That rule decides what counts as a canary pass, so it goes to the owner for ratification (brief item 6).

The pre-registered K6a rule was not triggered (0 of 60 announced), so the creation-time regression family stays in the M3 catalogue. The tap sees what NVDA queues, with priorities and global cancels, so every K7a and K7b attempt was classified without the GPL add-on: D2's add-on rule is not triggered. For polite live regions filled after page load, fills in the same frame are not announced and fills from 50 ms are. That contradicts DR-0037's 150 ms post-load boundary; the 350 ms pre-load boundary was not tested (brief item 4).
