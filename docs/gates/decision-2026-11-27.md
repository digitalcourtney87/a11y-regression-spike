## 1. Summary and recommendation

**Technical recommendation: proceed, in a scoped form** (CASCADE in the PRD §42 vocabulary; the [addendum](../decision-2026-11-27-addendum.md) moves the technical reading to PIVOT).

- **Phase 0's own question is answered yes.** It asked whether each instrument can measure what the comparison needs. The speech instrument passed G1 with 0 failures in 252 valid gating-canary runs. The event instruments passed G2 with 0 signature failures in 260.
- **The confirmatory results at 77 patterns support three of the four hypotheses.** H1 holds outright. H2 and H3 hold under the UNION combination rule only.
- **H4's reliability targets are met.** The test journeys show reachability limits, though, which are an operational risk.

The evidence supports a design in which event observation (B2) is the core detector and real NVDA runs selectively at triggered steps (Arm D). It does not support claiming NVDA's value under adjudication, which remains unsettled. If you proceed, start by making journeys reliably reachable, before any larger confirmatory study.

A *stop* would also be defensible on technical grounds alone. Real NVDA's addition is about 5 points, with an interval reaching down to 0.9 points. The hardest symptoms (focus escaping or not being restored) remain weak for every arm. Whether those margins are worth building on is a commercial judgement outside this repository.

## 3. Failure taxonomy and limits

**Where the arms differ.** The breakdown by symptom is from the 59-pattern round in [G4 §4](G4.md).

- axe detects mainly names.
- The tree adds roles, states, traps and blocked journeys.
- Events add duplicated and missing announcements.
- NVDA's additions concentrate in missing announcements and focus restoration.
- FOCUS_ESCAPES_DIALOG (1 of 4) and FOCUS_NOT_RESTORED (at most 1 of 4) stay weak for every arm.

**Baseline reachability.** Four of the 18 second-round patterns failed a baseline goal in every repetition, and a fifth (date-picker) failed with the wrong symptom. Read-only diagnosis and two bounded calibration studies explain most of these:

| Pattern | Cause established | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Bootstrap modal | The goal check runs before focus moves, so it is a sampling-time effect (M8) | Timing control needed in any next study |
| Vuetify input | The page really exposes an unnamed field while focused, and NVDA says "edit blank". Owned markup reproduces this (M9) | A journey-goal limit, not a reader fault |
| Calendar, Carbon | Expected content is present, but NVDA's fixed browse sequence produces no speech. Calendar's content sits inside an `application`-role container; no such role is asserted for Carbon | **Unresolved.** NVDA's internal mode is unobserved |
| Date-picker | The goal reaches the defect before the expectation does | A journey-design rule |
| Owned controls (no corpus pattern) | The simulated cursor stalled on unbacked text | Fixed by cursor v2, which is registered for future studies only (M9, DR-0122) |

**Further limits:**
- The corpus is 51 seeded and 26 mined regressions in two SPA contexts plus open-source libraries. It does not estimate how common these regressions are in the wild.
- The second round was built after first-round outcomes were known; this is disclosed in A-004–018.
- There is one environment: `windows-2025` image 20260925.250.1, Chrome 153, NVDA 2026.2 with eSpeak NG.
- There is one screen reader. Speech is measured as queued text, not audio, and queued-then-cancelled text counts as spoken.
- M7 is reproducible only from the `protocol-freeze-v4` tag. Later harness work has moved the hash on main.

## 6. Risks and scope changes

- **Journey maintenance.** Real-NVDA journeys can fail on a working page because of timing, focus-dependent naming or browse context. A production design would need authoring rules and calibration controls (M8, M9).
- **Unsettled adjudicated value.** NVDA's value under adjudication is unsettled. Any claim needs a new pre-registered study on fresh patterns, not a third expansion of this corpus.
- **Generalisation.** One screen reader, one browser and one image. Results may not carry over to JAWS, VoiceOver, mobile or other Chrome versions.
- **Scope changes made during the spike** (all recorded):
  - The 18-pattern second round (DR-0092 to DR-0110).
  - The exploratory M8 calibration (DR-0114 to DR-0118).
  - The registered M9 evaluation (DR-0120 to DR-0122).

  None changed an M7 verdict.
